# PlotMap — 5-Minute Dealer Demo Script

For showing PlotMap to an Indian property dealer. Keep it fast, visual, and about
_their_ daily work — not features. The map is the hero.

---

## Before you start (10 seconds)

- Open the **Client Presentation**: `/app/plotmap/`
- Have the **New Chandigarh** map ready — it's marked **★ Recommended** and is the
  one with live highlights. This is your "wow" map.

---

## The flow (about 5 minutes)

### 1. Open with the map (60s) — "This is what your client sees"
- Tap **New Chandigarh** (the Recommended tile).
- Let the masterplan load: glowing roads, raised 3D blocks.
- Tap a **road** → it lights up. Tap a **block** → it lifts.
- Tap **A / B / C / D** → whole zones highlight at once.
- Line: _"Aap client ko poora area ek dum saaf, live map pe dikhate ho — na
  PowerPoint, na paper."_

### 2. Show a property (60s) — "From map to plot in one tap"
- Go to **Properties** → open one property.
- Point at the four buttons (all visible, no scrolling):
  **View on Masterplan · View on Sector Map · Show Area Context · Send via WhatsApp.**
- Tap **View on Masterplan** → it opens _right here_, with a close button. No page jump.
- Line: _"Client ka focus kabhi break nahi hota — sab kuch isi screen pe."_

### 3. Explain WhatsApp sharing (45s) — "Send it before they leave"
- Tap **Send via WhatsApp**.
- WhatsApp opens with a clean, ready message (plot, size, area, live map link).
- Line: _"Ek tap mein client ko property + live map ka link chala jaata hai.
  Price ya internal detail kabhi share nahi hoti — sirf client-safe cheezein."_

### 4. Show the staff side (60s) — "Your team runs the day from here"
- Open **Team Workspace** (`/admin/team.html`).
- Point at: **Add Client**, **Add Property**, **Open Map Studio**, **My Clients**,
  **Recent Activity**.
- Line: _"Staff ke liye simple work table — client add karo, property add karo,
  map pe mark karo. Bas."_

### 5. Show the owner dashboard (45s) — "You see the whole business in 2 seconds"
- Open **Dashboard** (`/admin/owner.html`).
- Point at **"Needs your attention today"** and **Property readiness**
  (Ready to show / Needs photo / Not on map yet).
- Line: _"Subah kholo, aur 2 second mein pata — aaj kya karna hai, kaunsi property
  ready hai, kaunsi client ko photo chahiye."_

---

## Dealer objections — quick answers

**"Kaam to chal raha hai."**
> _"Bilkul. Ye usi kaam ko tez aur premium banata hai. Jo aap abhi phone pe
> photos aur PDF bhej ke karte ho, wahi ek live map pe 10 second mein ho jaata
> hai — client ko yaad reh jaata hai."_

**"Mujhe software ki zarurat nahi."**
> _"Ye software jaisa lagta hi nahi. Client ko ek sundar map dikhta hai, aapko ek
> simple dashboard. Koi training nahi chahiye — jaise WhatsApp use karte ho waise
> hi."_

**"Client ko kya farak padega?"**
> _"Client ko trust aata hai. Official map pe exact plot, sector proof, aur ek
> professional presentation — competitor abhi bhi screenshot bhej raha hoga."_

**"Mere staff ko use karna mushkil hoga?"**
> _"Team Workspace ek work table hai — Add Client, Add Property, Map Studio. Teen
> button. Naya banda bhi pehle din se use kar lega."_

**"Kitna time lagega?"**
> _"Aaj hi. New Chandigarh map ready hai highlights ke saath. Baaki maps proof-map
> ki tarah abhi bhi chalte hain; jab chaaho Map Studio mein mark kar do — 5 minute
> mein ek sector."_

---

## Golden rules for the demo

- Lead with the **map**, not the menus.
- Always demo on the **Recommended (golden) map** for highlights.
- Never open billing/settings in front of a client — that lives in the owner drawer.
- Everything the client sees is **client-safe** — say it out loud, it builds trust.
